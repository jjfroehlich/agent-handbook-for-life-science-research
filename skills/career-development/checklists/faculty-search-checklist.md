# Faculty Search Checklist

Use this before or during a faculty or group-leader search to audit readiness and package coherence.

Use the relevant stage/role checks, not a universal eligibility score. Paper counts, venues, awards, age, search months or application volume do not by themselves establish readiness or predict selection.

## Readiness Audit

- Independent research identity in one sentence.
- Future program with clear intellectual ownership, including the boundaries of any continuing mentor collaboration; it can remain in the same field.
- Evidence of execution: papers, methods, funding, collaborations, mentoring, or field-building.
- Fit targets by institution type, department needs, resources, and teaching expectations.
- Timeline for letters, statements, talks, visits, and references.
- Mentor or peer feedback on the package before submission.

## Package Coherence

- CV supports the research and teaching case.
- Cover letter explains specific institutional fit.
- Research statement shows future questions, feasibility, first projects, funding path, and local resource fit.
- Teaching or mentoring statement uses concrete evidence when requested.
- Diversity, service, or outreach material is included only when requested or expected and is evidence-based.
- Teaching/mentoring/inclusion examples identify actual learners, methods and reflection; proposed courses or activities remain plans. Teaching-heavy roles may need a fuller portfolio and feasible undergraduate research.

## Interview Preparation

- Job talk: clear problem, contribution, evidence, and future direction.
- Chalk talk: feasible first projects, alternatives, resources, personnel, and grant logic.
- One-on-ones: tailored questions for faculty, trainees, administrators, and collaborators.
- Difficult questions: independence, funding risk, failed projects, and fit tradeoffs.
- Reciprocal inquiry: trainee voice, recent-hire experience, facility capacity/fees, leadership and evaluation authority; voluntary choices about personal questions and access needs.

## Offer and Startup Check

- People, salary, space, equipment, start date, moving support, teaching relief, protected time, and written terms.
- Must-haves versus tradeable items.
- Rationale for each ask tied to execution risk.
- Core user fees and capacity, salary period/grant share and benefits, fund release/expiry/restrictions, purchasing authority, teaching/service timing and renewal/promotion duties.
- Material revisions confirmed by the authorized decision maker, rather than assuming an intermediate or signed letter settles all conditions.
- Accepted-position lead times and permissions for facilities, hiring, teaching, award deadlines, housing and prior-lab data/material/manuscript/account handover.

## Output

Select the readiness gaps, package edits, interview preparation, or offer questions relevant to the user's current stage.
