# Application Review Checklist

Use this for CVs, resumes, cover letters, faculty materials, postdoc outreach, and other career artifacts.

Select checks for the requested scope. A local bullet edit does not require a whole application audit or an outside reviewer. Use supplied facts first; ask only consequential missing questions.

## First Pass

- Identify the target role, reader, document type, stage, and deadline.
- State the reader's likely decision question in one sentence.
- Name the strongest evidence already present.
- Name the most important missing or buried evidence.
- Check whether the document follows the right genre for the audience.

## Evidence Check

- Does the opening third show the most relevant evidence?
- Are claims backed by outputs, actions, decisions, results, or examples?
- Does each bullet show the user's contribution rather than only lab context?
- Are unfamiliar terms explained while specifically requested technical tools/methods remain visible?
- Are metrics honest and supplied by the user?
- Do publication, presentation, patent, award, training and language labels state the actual status and individual role?

## Structure Check

- Academic CV: comprehensive scholarly record, easy section scanning, complete chronology where expected.
- Industry resume: selected evidence, role language, concise bullets, relevant skills, and outcomes.
- Cover letter: specific role, criteria match, evidence, fit, and concise close.
- Faculty package: coherent research identity, future program, institutional fit, and resources needed.

## Bullet Rewrite Pattern

Use `action + individual contribution + method or scope + result + relevance`.

Avoid inventing metrics. If no result is supplied, ask for one or use a truthful scope, output, or decision.

## Cover Letter Final Pass

- Uses the reader's language accurately.
- Names the specific role, lab, department, or organization.
- Gives concrete examples rather than adjectives.
- Explains fit, retaining useful overlap so the letter and CV are separately intelligible.
- Has a clear purpose and practical close; a useful conventional opening need not be replaced.
- Includes personal context only by the applicant's bounded choice or an actual applicable requirement.

## Output

Prioritize the fixes that matter for the requested document or passage, and provide useful targeted rewrites. Do not require three problems when fewer are present.

## Recommendation Letter Scaffold Final Pass

- Opportunity, level, criteria, and deadline are explicit.
- Recommender relationship and basis for judgment are clear.
- Specific contributions, outputs, role, and impact are included.
- Attributes are tied to evidence rather than unsupported praise.
- Language is calibrated to career stage and opportunity.
- Candidate-supplied facts are accurate and private jokes/unsupported praise removed; the writer retains any honest qualifications.
- Final endorsement strength, comparative rank, and recommender voice are left to the writer.
- The writer actually knows the cited work and is willing to provide the requested reference; instructions, deadline and confirmations are tracked without coaching false answers.
