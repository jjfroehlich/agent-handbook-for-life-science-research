# Poster Review Checklist

Use before printing, uploading, or presenting a scientific poster.

Apply the checks needed for the requested scope and venue; a title edit does not require a full presenter-preparation package.

## Venue And Format

- Poster size, orientation, margin, and upload requirements are known.
- The design fits print, static digital, or interactive digital use.
- Actual final-size viewing or expected zoom has been checked; a small proof alone cannot certify it. If only a plan was supplied, readability remains unverified.
- Required identification and credit are present; contact exposure and optional personal details fit permission and purpose.

## Main Message

- The title or top visual area signals the main finding, question, or takeaway.
- A passerby has an entry point, and a close reader can decode the claim and its evidence without depending on an absent presenter.
- Decisive evidence receives enough space; several findings or alternatives can remain equally inspectable when comparison is the task.
- Background and methods are only as detailed as the viewer needs.

## Layout And Reading Path

- The reading path is obvious.
- Block relationships and reading order fit the actual content, without a prescribed count or geometry.
- Headings help readers find the information; required or useful research-section names remain valid.
- Whitespace and grouping separate ideas.
- No text block looks like a manuscript paragraph pasted onto the poster.

## Figures And Text

- Paper figures have been redrawn, cropped, enlarged, or relabeled for poster viewing.
- Legends are shortened and labels are near the data.
- Abbreviations are limited or explained.
- Font sizes and contrast survive a distance check.
- QR-linked or handout material is used for optional detail instead of crowding the poster.
- Decisive controls, limitations, labels, units and interval definitions are visible with the inference, including on a live conversation poster.
- Digital media have a useful role and a stop/pause plan; newcomers retain stationary topic, motivation, method context and key findings. Static digital display is valid.
- Actual event, template, printer, color/material and transport requirements govern production; archived software/vendor recommendations and fixed dpi values are not guarantees.
- Asset reuse permission and any implemented link/access path are checked; acknowledgment, free availability and a QR graphic alone establish neither.

## Presenter Prep

- When presenting in conversation, prepare a brief orientation and a longer explanation if the setting calls for both.
- Likely questions have concise answers.
- Conversation and silent reading are both available as appropriate; deeper questions need not imply poor design. An informative visit needs no compulsory ask or follow-up.

## Highest-Leverage Fixes

Choose among these repairs by the most consequential current failure; a sound title does not need rewriting before an unreadable decisive comparison is fixed.

- Repair an unclear or overstated title/claim when it misdirects readers.
- Enlarge or clarify the decisive evidence and required control when the comparison cannot be inspected.
- Shorten secondary background or procedures when they obscure the account, retaining information that changes interpretation.
- Repair reading order, labels or contrast when visitors cannot locate or decode the evidence at the intended viewing distance.
