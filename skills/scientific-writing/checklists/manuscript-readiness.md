# Manuscript Readiness Checklist

Use for whole-paper or section-level readiness review. Select checks relevant to the requested scope; a local wording edit does not require a full manuscript audit.

## Structure

- The manuscript makes its contribution or organizing purpose visible; related contributions need not be forced into one hypothesis.
- Each major result connects to a question, hypothesis, prediction, or task.
- Section jobs are distinct: setup, reproducibility, evidence, interpretation, and implications are not blurred.
- Headings, topic sentences, and figure titles help readers recover the story.
- The order makes the evidence and dependencies understandable. Chronological reporting can be appropriate when sequence matters or the destination requires it.
- Paragraphs follow context-content-conclusion or another clear topic/evidence/takeaway logic.
- Paragraphs have a clear point and support, with a closing inference or transition where needed.
- Details are placed where readers need them; moving supporting detail must not hide evidence essential to a claim or leave it inaccessible.

## Section Logic

- The introduction establishes the relevant context, actual need or question, and contribution without manufacturing a knowledge gap or imposing one opening sequence.
- Results paragraphs state findings and evidence before broad interpretation.
- Results contain comparison, direction or qualitative pattern, evidence type, and figure/table reference when supplied.
- Figures or tables have a clear role in the evidence spine, including descriptive resources or workflows when central to the contribution.
- The discussion answers the research question and calibrates implications.
- Limitations name where results do and do not apply.
- Figures/legends supply enough local context for inspection, with accurate finding or neutral titles and stage/venue-dependent explanation. Missing metadata remain flagged; necessary exact values stay accessible.
- Thesis chapters fit the applicable degree/programme/cohort instructions and form a coherent contribution; chapter counts, separate endings and paper-adaptation formats are not assumed universal.

## Claim Calibration

- Causal language matches the design.
- Scope, population, system, and uncertainty are explicit.
- Limitations bound interpretation instead of apologizing for the study.
- Future work separates extensions of an established contribution from evidence still needed for a current claim; genuine gaps are identified and unsupported claims narrowed.

## Prose

- Vague nouns and weak verbs are replaced where specificity improves accuracy.
- Long sentences are split when they hide actor, action, or claim.
- Base clauses are recoverable before modifiers and caveats are added.
- Comparable findings use parallel syntax unless a real scientific distinction requires different form.
- One term is used for one concept unless a distinction is intended.
- Jargon and abbreviations are defined only when needed.
- Style edits preserve meaning, caveats, and author voice.

## Authorship And Acknowledgements

- Contribution language names concrete roles.
- Roles, eligibility and order are checked separately under the applicable policy; substantial contributors have a real opportunity to meet drafting/revision and approval criteria where required.
- Acknowledgements are not settled merely because a contribution was called help, funding, design advice or feedback; check other qualifying work and unresolved criteria first.
- AI disclosure describes actual assistance and human checks only, in the required location. Do not assert all-author review/approval or tool use without facts; tools are not authors under the guidance used here.
- Private people or participants are named only when appropriate and permitted.
