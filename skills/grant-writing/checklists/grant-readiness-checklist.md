# Grant Readiness Checklist

Use this for whole-application readiness. For a bounded section review, apply only relevant checks and mark unassessed items rather than requiring a complete audit.

Distinguish what the supplied documents show from unverified rules, figures, permissions, reader feedback or performed submission. A clean draft alone cannot certify scientific validity, actual access or approval.

## Funder Fit And Compliance

- The opportunity funds this type of work.
- Current eligibility, location, career-stage, budget, and deadline rules are checked.
- Required headings, templates, page limits, formatting, and portal fields are followed.
- Each assessor criterion maps to a findable section or label.

## Narrative And Aims

- The objective appears early and is concise.
- The active premise connects the problem, aims, approach, and expected payoff.
- The first page makes the problem matter.
- Aims are independently valuable where possible.
- Aim dependencies fit the scheme and have appropriate evidence, qualification milestones, decision gates, or credible alternatives.
- The project is grouped into a reviewer-manageable number of goals or aims.

## Significance, Innovation, And Approach

- Significance explains field state, unmet need and why the work matters, including timing when it changes the case.
- Innovation names what is new and why that novelty enables the work.
- Approach sections include rationale, plan, interpretable outcomes, risks, and alternatives.
- An ambitious scientific question has a credible possible contribution; feasibility evidence or an assessable development/qualification plan supports the actual funded purpose. Missing access is not itself desirable scientific risk.

## Feasibility And Budget

- Preliminary data or examples reduce specific reviewer concerns.
- Budget and allowed costs support the promised work.
- CV, team, environment, or institutional evidence supports execution capacity; untested capabilities remain explicit.
- Completed work is not presented as future funded work.
- Project-change or resubmission issues have a support plan.

## Reviewer Readability

- Headings, topic sentences, figures, and intentional emphasis form a coherent skim path.
- Figures are legible, aligned, high-resolution, and tied to claims.
- Non-specialist reviewers get enough context to score the proposal.
- Available consenting readers supply complementary scientific/readability critique while useful revisions are possible; actual feedback is distinguished from a plan to request it.

## Failure Signals

- Out-of-scope project.
- Buried objective.
- Generic significance or novelty.
- Unexplained or unjustified dependencies without evidence or an assessable development plan.
- Boilerplate impact, DEI, CV, or budget sections.
- Hidden risks or missing alternatives.
- Altered templates or hard-to-find compliance answers.

## Timeline And Funder-Fit Addendum

- Preparation allows the feasible critique and authorization needed by actual deadlines; no fixed two-month or rest interval is imposed on a useful local repair.
- Critical readers include specialist, adjacent, and non-specialist perspectives where useful.
- Submission is not being saved for the final deadline rush without reason.
- Objective, novelty and critical context are visible early; urgency appears only when supported and relevant.
- Protocol choices, controls, analyses, and references are explained.
- Budget derives from actual work and eligible categories; previous awards provide context rather than set the price or justify omitting essential costs.
- Every major expense has a justification.
- If there was an LOI, scope or amount changes are explained and checked against the applicable process.
- Subheads, visuals, and captions help evaluators find significance.
- Future ramifications are stated without overclaiming.

## Application-to-award handoff, when relevant

- Selection, authorized agreement/costs and technical/resource readiness are separate confirmed or unresolved states.
- Responsible institutional signatories and scientific/administrative/financial owners are identified under the actual award form.
- Required secure records, public/confidential outputs, data-management/access, acknowledgment, reporting and approval/payment dependencies have owners and actual schedules.
- Partner contributions, compatible endpoints, financial/access/credit and departure arrangements are reciprocal working commitments rather than inferred from a template alone.
- A consequential deviation has its scientific/resource effects and actual change route identified; no proposed amendment or officer discussion is reported as approved.
