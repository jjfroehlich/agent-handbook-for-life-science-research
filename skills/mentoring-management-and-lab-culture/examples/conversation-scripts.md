# Conversation Scripts

Use a script as a starting point for the actual relationship. It is proposed wording, not evidence that a conversation or agreement occurred.

## Completed fictional case: resetting a submission expectation

**Given.** Lina supervises research assistant Omar. Two sample sheets requested for Tuesday noon arrived Wednesday morning, and one omitted the new batch field. Analyst Priya could not perform Tuesday's planned check. Lina had introduced the template without training Omar and assigned imaging preparation over the same Tuesday morning. Omar reports the competing assignments; no health condition, motive, sample loss or biological result is given. The ordinary work discussion is safe in this fictional setup.

**Prepared conversation.** “The last two sheets arrived Wednesday, and one lacked the batch field, so Priya could not do Tuesday's check. I assigned competing work and did not arrange template training. Can we walk through where the task became blocked? I propose that submission takes priority Tuesday morning, with imaging preparation moved to Wednesday. I will work through the template with you Monday. Would that make Tuesday noon workable, or is another constraint missing?”

**Agreed record after the conversation.** Omar and Lina agree to that priority change. Lina will provide Monday training; Omar will prepare the Tuesday sheet and flag a blocker before noon; Priya will check the required fields. They set a two-submission review. This records a work arrangement, not a performance or misconduct finding.

**Given follow-up.** Both later sheets contain the required fields; one arrives on time and one is delayed by equipment access, with notice before noon. The review retains the template support and asks Lina to resolve the access dependency. It does not conclude that Omar lacks motivation or that sequencing succeeded.

**Changed condition.** If Omar instead reports a threat to his reference for seeking advice, the routine summary-to-supervisor pattern no longer follows automatically. Prepare an independent request for advice that first asks about remit, confidentiality and reporting limits; do not require confrontation or disclose identifying details in a group review.

## Unclear Expectation Feedback

"I realize we have not made the deadline standard explicit. Let's reset it together. The pattern I have seen is ____. What constraints or misunderstandings should I know about? Going forward, the expectation is ____. I will provide ____. Let's check back on ____."

Use when a trainee missed deadlines, meetings, documentation, authorship duties, or communication norms that were implicit.

## Claim-Focused Criticism

"The concern is with the claim that ____. The evidence currently supports ____, but not ____. The assumption I want you to test is ____. A stronger next version would ____."

Use when feedback must be rigorous without becoming personal.

## Distress-Support Conversation

"I have noticed ____. What practical support would be useful right now? You do not need to share a diagnosis with me. We can discuss changes within my role and suitable support services. Before sharing personal details, check each service's confidentiality and reporting limits. Here is what I can and cannot keep private in my role."

Use only for non-clinical support. Route to qualified help for clinical, safety, harassment, retaliation, or emergency concerns.

## Repair After Reactive Feedback

"I want to revisit how I handled our last conversation. My feedback should have been clearer and less reactive. The substantive issue is ____. What impact did the conversation have, and what would make our next feedback discussion more useful?"

Use when the mentor's delivery damaged trust but the work issue still needs attention.

## Co-Mentoring Route

"This issue would benefit from advice beyond me. I can help identify people with relevant expertise, and you can seek independent advice. Each adviser should explain their own privacy and reporting limits. We can coordinate practical work changes you choose to bring back, without making your independent advice depend on my approval."

Use when the PI lacks expertise, has a conflict of interest, or the trainee needs independent advice.

## Team Facilitation And Conflict Scripts

Speak-last facilitation:

```text
Before I give my view, I want to hear two interpretations of these data.
What am I missing?
Who sees this differently?
What would you try next, and why?
```

Proposal-before-advice:

```text
Bring me the next step you propose and why. Name the main uncertainty and any credible alternative or stopping condition that matters. If you are too new to propose a step yet, we can work through an example together.
```

SBIF feedback:

```text
Situation:
Behavior:
Impact:
Future:
Support or constraint to discuss:
```

Non-coercive social invitation:

```text
This is optional and not a measure of commitment. We will also create work-hours ways for people to connect and share information.
```

Team conflict opening:

```text
Let's separate the scientific disagreement from the working relationship. First, what claim is each person making? Second, what decision do we need? Third, what process will we use if we still disagree?
```
