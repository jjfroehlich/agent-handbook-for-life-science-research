# Lab Handbook Checklist

Choose sections for recurring shared decisions; a narrow policy request needs only relevant items.

## Possible Sections

- Mission and behavioral values.
- Roles and expectations by member type.
- Onboarding flow and first-month contacts.
- Meeting types, cadence, and agenda ownership.
- Communication channels, response windows, and after-hours norms.
- Working hours, flexibility, holidays, vacations, and deadline exceptions.
- Authorship, credit, acknowledgments, and contribution tracking.
- Data, code, notebooks, storage, access, sharing, handoff, and departure norms.
- AI task knowledge, checking, local learning exercises and human accountability; separately confirmed privacy, approved-tool and disclosure requirements.
- Safety, conduct, mistakes, help-seeking, and escalation routes.
- Resources, institutional contacts, and support routes with verified confidentiality/reporting limits.
- Handbook owner, update cadence, and change log.

## Quality Checks

- A policy creating obligations states a usable default, responsibility and relevant exception or concern route. Include maintenance details where they help; every section need not repeat every field.
- Sensitive sections avoid promises beyond institutional authority.
- Practical questions are answered directly, not hidden behind values language.
- Role-specific differences are explicit.
- Public documentation, internal records and delivered practices are distinguished; neither a reading acknowledgement nor an engagement badge proves agreement, implementation or culture quality.
- Choose a feasible co-writing or initial-draft/consultation process. Link authoritative rules and say when a matter has no general lab policy.
- Records preserve raw inputs, analysis order, traceable corrections and departure retrieval under actual consent/access/retention terms; a local release deadline is not universal.
- Public-facing versions omit private names, source provenance, and internal-only details.
