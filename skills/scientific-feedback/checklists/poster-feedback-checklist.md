# Poster Feedback Checklist

Use this for scientific poster drafts before print, upload, presentation, or conference feedback.

Select checks for the actual format and requested scope; a static or unattended poster need not include a live walkthrough, and text alone cannot certify layout or standing-distance readability.

## Checks

- The poster makes its purpose and important claim or connected questions visible; a research briefing need not force distinct decisions into one finding.
- Title, section headings, and layout support a fast walk-up read.
- Figures carry the evidence and are readable at standing distance.
- Text supports the intended encounter, including self-contained interpretation when no presenter is there; essential decoding and qualifications remain visible.
- Secondary methods/details are compressed or moved when they compete with the evidence; retain necessary controls, comparisons and qualifications.
- Color, hierarchy, labels, and flow do not require a guided tour to understand.
- If live presentation is part of the task, prepare a proportionate opening and likely question answers without imposing a fixed duration.

## Failure signals

- The poster is a manuscript wall.
- Secondary background or methods crowd the decisive comparison or explanation; a methods poster can legitimately foreground its method.
- Feedback misses the consequential current reading problem, whether wording, evidence, hierarchy or audience path.

## Output reminder

Return the relevant argument, evidence and visual findings at the requested depth. Standing-distance or layout judgments require actual visual inspection; a walkthrough is useful when delivery is part of the request.
